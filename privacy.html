Privacy · Effective October 24, 2026

Privacy, in plain language.

Loonie - Split Bill is designed to calculate a shared bill on your device. The app currently has no login or account, GPS location, cloud backend, subscription, or in-app purchase.

Who this covers

This policy describes the current version of Loonie - Split Bill for people using the app, including in Canada, the United States, and China. It should be updated if the app’s data practices or features change.

Information you enter

Bill amounts and other entries you provide are used by the app to perform its calculations. The core calculation is designed to run locally on your device. There is no account or cloud backend for syncing this information, and the app does not use GPS location.

Information collected by the developer

Based on the app’s current design, the developer does not receive bill entries through a developer-operated server. This statement does not describe information that Apple or your device may process independently under their own services and policies.

Sharing and third parties

The app has no developer-operated cloud service for sharing your bill data. This policy does not claim that any optional device or operating-system feature is used. If you choose to use a device feature to share information, review the destination and that service’s privacy terms before sending.

Retention and deletion

There is no developer account or server copy of bill entries to delete. Any information kept on your device is subject to the app’s local storage behavior and your device settings. Removing the app may remove its locally stored data; check your device before deleting anything you need.

Children

Loonie is a bill-splitting utility and is not designed to request personal information from children. If you believe personal information was sent to support, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app changes. The effective date above shows when this version was prepared. Please revisit this page for the latest policy.
