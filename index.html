01 / THE APP

One bill.
Clear shares.

Enter the bill details, choose how many people are sharing, and use the result to settle up together. Loonie is designed to do its core bill calculations on your device, without an account.

02 / NEED A HAND?

Quick answers

A

How to use Loonie

  1. Enter the meal amount from your bill.
  2. Add the tax and tip amounts or percentages you want included.
  3. Set the number of people sharing.
  4. Review the total and per-person share, then settle up with your group.

Your version of the app may use slightly different labels or steps.

B

Can we split unevenly?

Use the equal-share calculation as a guide. If your group has different orders or wants a custom split, agree on those amounts together and adjust outside the app.

C

Which currency is supported?

Check the currency shown in your app and enter amounts consistently. Loonie calculates the numbers you enter; it does not exchange currencies.

D

Does everyone need the app?

No account or shared online group is needed for the core calculation. You can read the result to your group or share it yourself.

03 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Try this first.

A quick check often gets the numbers back on track.

  • The total looks offCheck the meal amount, tax, tip, and number of people. Make sure you have not added tax or tip twice.
  • The per-person amount has a few cents differenceSome totals cannot divide evenly to the cent. Agree who will cover the rounding difference.
  • The app is not respondingClose and reopen the app, then check that your device has the latest version available to you.
  • You still need helpContact support below and include your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please do not send payment card details or other sensitive information.
